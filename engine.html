NE-02 · The Engine · half two of two — the action

The engine is what acts.

Separate machine, separate job. The brain holds the data; the engine is a set of agents that arrive already loaded — skills installed, MCP servers connected, workflows agreed in advance — and work that data on a schedule, then report back.

  • preloaded skills
  • MCP connections
  • predetermined workflows
  • scheduled runs
  • approval gate
  • reports back

PLATE 01 / 06EXPLODED

  1. Plate 01 · The feed

    It reads from the brain, not the internet.

    Every run starts with your own structured records as context. That's the whole reason the brain comes first.

  2. Plate 02 · MCP servers

    The systems it can touch, wired in advance.

    Email, calendar, CRM, billing, databases, internal tools — each exposed as a defined set of callable operations. Nothing improvised, nothing undocumented.

  3. Plate 03 · Skills

    Your playbooks, installed as capabilities.

    "Triage the shared inbox." "Reconcile these invoices." Written once, reviewed like code, then permanently available to the agent.

  4. Plate 04 · The agents

    One operator, helpers for the big jobs.

    The operator plans and delegates; helper agents run the parallel work. They arrive configured — there is no setup phase on your side.

  5. Plate 05 · The approval gate

    It reads, analyzes and drafts on its own.

    Sending, publishing, buying, deleting — every outbound action waits for your one-word approval. It never types your passwords.

  6. Plate 06 · The report

    It tells you what it did.

    Each run ends with a written account — what ran, what it found, what's waiting on you — in the chat app you already use.

  7. Engine running

    Assembled, it runs unattended.

    On your server, on a schedule, on your data. You approve what leaves.

NE-02 · §01Day to day

What it actually does.

CAP 01

Inbox & comms

Reads, triages, summarizes, drafts replies — where you already work.

CAP 02

Documents → structured data

Turns PDFs, spreadsheets, whole folders into clean records; compares figures — "is this quote better than what we pay?"

CAP 03

Scheduled, unattended work

Recurring reports, digests, reminders in plain language — "every weekday 9am, post an inbox summary to Slack."

CAP 04

Monitoring & alerts

Watches inboxes, portals, feeds, systems; surfaces only what changed and needs you.

CAP 05

Business-app upkeep

Connects to email, calendar, CRM, docs, billing, databases; looks things up, logs activity, keeps records current.

CAP 06

Playbooks on real tools

Researches with sources, operates a browser like a person (portals with no API), runs code to crunch data, drafts documents — end to end.

CAP 07

Memory that compounds

Permanent memory of your business + a log of everything it's done; proven methods become reusable skills, so it gets faster the longer it runs.

NE-02 · §02The gate

Human-in-the-loop by default.

It reads, analyzes, and drafts on its own. Sending, publishing, buying, or deleting waits for your one-word approval.

Every outbound action queues at the gate. You say "send" — it sends. You say nothing — it waits. And it never types your passwords.
