Discovery call · 30 minutes · free · with Michael

Pick a time.

Bring the two processes that eat the most hours. We map where the data lives, sketch what an agent would actually do with it, and you get a straight answer about whether it's worth building. If it isn't, you hear that on this call.

What happens on the call.

  1. 01

    Your bottlenecks

    You describe the recurring work. No deck, no discovery questionnaire — just the tasks that repeat.

  2. 02

    Where the data lives

    We map the systems: what has an API, what doesn't, what's trapped in documents.

  3. 03

    Two or three workflows

    We pick the first candidates and describe what the agent would do, step by step, and where it would stop for approval.

  4. 04

    The honest read

    Whether this pays for itself, roughly what it takes, and the shape of the price: one-time setup plus monthly operation.
