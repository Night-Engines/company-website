What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser, so it can recognise the browser on a later visit. Browsers can also store data in other ways, such as "local storage". This notice covers both. A first-party cookie comes from the site you're on. A third-party cookie comes from another company's service embedded in that site.

What this site uses

Night Engines runs no analytics, no advertising and no social-media tracking. There are only two items, and the second one is off until you say yes. It belongs to Calendly, not to us: we don't receive the data its tools collect.

Your choices

  • On this site: you choose on your first visit. To change your mind, use Cookie settings in the footer of any page. If you switch from Accept to Reject, the page reloads without Calendly.
