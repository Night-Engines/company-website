Privacy policy.

Last updated 2026-10-07

Who we are

Night Engines is the trading name of Michael Angeles, Athens, Greece. Michael is the controller of the personal data described here: he decides why and how it is used. There is no separate data protection officer; questions go straight to him at michael.angeles@nightengines.com.

This policy covers nightengines.com and the conversations that start from it. Client projects run under their own written agreement.

The short version

  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking pixels, no newsletter.
  • We hold what you choose to send: your emails and, if you book a call, your booking details.
  • The site's fonts and code are served from our own domain. The booking calendar (Calendly) is the one third-party service, and it loads only if you allow it.
  • We never sell or rent your data, and we don't use it for profiling or automated decisions.

What we collect, why, and on what legal basis

When you email us

Your name, email address, and whatever you write, including details about your business. We use it to reply, to scope and quote work you ask about, and to keep a record of the conversation. Basis: steps you ask us to take before a possible contract (GDPR art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (art. 6(1)(f)).

When you book a call

The booking page asks for your name, email address, the time you pick, and any answers you give to the booking questions. Calendly collects these for us and sends us a notification. We use them to hold the meeting and prepare for it. Basis: steps before a possible contract (art. 6(1)(b)). The calendar only loads after you agree to Calendly's cookies (art. 6(1)(a), see Cookies). If you'd rather not, email us and we'll find a time by hand.

When you visit the site

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address for security purposes, whether or not you have a GitHub account. We don't receive or use these logs. Basis: our legitimate interest in running a secure website (art. 6(1)(f)).

The site also keeps your cookie choice in your browser's local storage, under the key ne-consent. It never leaves your device.

Transfers outside the EU

Google, Calendly and GitHub are based in the United States. Each is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework, which the European Commission has found gives an adequate level of protection (GDPR art. 45). Where a transfer isn't covered by the framework, the provider's Standard Contractual Clauses apply.

How long we keep it

  • Emails that don't lead to work: deleted within 24 months of our last exchange.
  • Calendly bookings: removed from Calendly within 12 months of the meeting.
  • Clients: records needed for invoicing and tax are kept as long as Greek tax and accounting law requires. Everything else is kept for the length of the engagement, plus 24 months.
  • Your cookie choice: stays in your browser until you clear it or change it.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of the data we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • restrict how we use it
  • send it to you or another provider in a portable format

You can also object to any use based on legitimate interest, and withdraw consent at any time. For cookies, use Cookie settings in the footer. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything done before you withdrew it.

To use any of these rights, email michael.angeles@nightengines.com. We'll answer within one month. If you think we've handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Hellenic Data Protection Authority (dpa.gr) or to the authority where you live. We'd appreciate the chance to fix it first.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies of its own. The booking calendar on the Book a call page is provided by Calendly, which sets its own cookies and loads its own analytics and marketing tools (for example Google Analytics and the Meta pixel). It loads only after you click Accept (or Allow Calendly). If you reject, nothing loads and no cookies are set. The full list is on our Cookie notice.

Security

Email and booking data sit with the providers above, behind their security and the access controls on our accounts. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach puts your data at risk, we'll tell you and the regulator as the GDPR requires.

Children

The site is for businesses. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Changes

If we change how we handle data, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change is significant and we have your email from an ongoing conversation, we'll tell you directly.
