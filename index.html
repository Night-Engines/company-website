CH 1 / 5 · The floor

One client. One server.

Every business we run gets its own sealed vault on dedicated hardware. Your data never mixes — not with other clients, not with anyone's cloud.

On screen — a row of sealed vaults in the dark. The lit one is yours.

CH 2 / 5 · The brain

First, we build the brain.

Docs, inboxes, spreadsheets, tribal knowledge — ingested, cleaned, and structured into records a model can actually use. One queryable source of truth, yours to keep.

On screen — scattered shards snap into an ordered lattice inside your vault.

CH 3 / 5 · The engine

Then the engine runs it.

AI agents arrive already set up for your business — tools connected, workflows agreed with you in advance — and work through the busywork: triage, records, reports, monitoring. Then they report back.

On screen — an agent core spins up; threads of work light the lattice.

CH 4 / 5 · The airlock

Nothing leaves without you.

Sending, publishing, buying — anything outbound queues at the door and waits for your one-word approval. It never types your passwords.

On screen — outbound items queue at the vault door; it opens only on approval.

CH 5 / 5 · Morning

By morning, it's done.

The vault is sealed again. Finished work is waiting — in the chat apps you already use: drafts, reports, alerts, records.

On screen — the sealed vault streams finished work to labeled destinations.
