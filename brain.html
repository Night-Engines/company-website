NE-01 · The Brain · half one of two — the data

The brain is the data half.

No agents here. This page is only about getting your business's knowledge into a shape a model can actually use — ingested, cleaned, structured, queryable. The engine is the other half, and it comes second on purpose.

CH 01 · THE MESS

Your knowledge is scattered by default.

Contracts in a drive, decisions in an inbox, numbers in a spreadsheet, history in a portal with no export — and the operating logic in your head. Nobody built it wrong; it accumulated.

Stage 01 — as found. Five sources, no index, nothing that can answer a question across all of them.

CH 02 · INGESTION

We ingest it and clean it.

PDFs and scans become records with fields. Folders become a system. Duplicates collapse, dates normalize, entities get resolved — the same document referenced three ways becomes one record. Tribal knowledge gets written down.

Stage 02 — the pass. The jittery traces straighten into clean, converging lines.

CH 03 · STRUCTURE

What comes out is queryable.

One indexed source of truth: search across everything at once, reports that pull from real records, and — later — the context an agent needs to act instead of guess. Plain, versioned files on your own server.

Stage 03 — the brain. The block fills; ask it anything and the answer carries its source.

The mess, as found.

Documents, inboxes, spreadsheets, exports, folders — and the operating knowledge that lives only in your head.

Where there's no API, we build the bridge: a custom extraction pass for scanned and photographed documents, or a server that exposes a legacy system as callable tools. Both are things we've shipped before — see the work.

NE-01 · §02What comes out

A business you can query.

Ask your business anything — get an answer with the record it came from.

Search and reporting across everything at once: contracts next to invoices next to the email thread that explains them. Valuable on its own, before any agent runs.

Why this comes first The brain is also what makes the engine safe: agents act on organized records, not guesses.

NE-01 · §03What you keep

Yours. Plain files. Portable.

The brain is version-controlled plain files on your own server — not rows in someone else's SaaS.

Walk away any day and it walks with you. No lock-in is a design decision, not a promise: the format itself makes leaving easy.
